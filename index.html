IThe origin

Your body is
the beginning.

Kage is an interactive experience based on the four elements: Fire, Water, Air, and Earth.

Using real-time body tracking and hand gestures, users can control and manipulate these elements through their movements.

A conversation between you and the forces of nature.

IIIThe experience

A movement.
A response.

Raising, lowering, and moving your hands changes the intensity, direction, and behaviour of the elements. Your body appears as a shadow on the screen, placing you inside the experience.
